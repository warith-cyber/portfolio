
# Modern Portfolio Starter

A clean multi-page portfolio in a glassmorphism UI style: frosted translucent panels over a luminous gradient backdrop, dark/light theme, and responsive layout.

## Quick start
1. Open `index.html` in your browser (no build tools required).
2. Replace **Your Name**, email, links, and texts.
3. Swap `/images/profile.svg` with your photo: use a square JPG/PNG named `profile.jpg` or keep the SVG.
4. Add your own screenshots in `/images/` and update `gallery.html` and `projects.html`.
5. To restyle, tweak the theme tokens at the top of `assets/styles.css`: `--accent-fill` (brand colour), `--mesh-a` to `--mesh-e` (background glow) and `--glass` / `--blur` (the frosted glass).

### CV PDF
- Your uploaded PDF is placed at: `assets/Web Portfolio_2025.pdf` (linked on the CV page). Replace it with your final CV if needed.

## Deploy
- Drag & drop the folder into Netlify, Vercel (as static), or GitHub Pages.
- Or zip the folder and upload to your host.

## Accessibility
- Skip link, proper landmarks, color-contrast friendly, keyboardable nav.
